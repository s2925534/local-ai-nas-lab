## Prompt

```
Draft a follow-up email from these meeting notes.

Notes:
<paste rough notes>

Recipients: <who this is going to>
Include: key decisions, action items with owners if known, and any open questions.
Keep it scannable — short paragraphs or bullets, not a wall of text.
```
